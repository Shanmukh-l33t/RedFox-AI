# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be free of harassment for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

This project builds offensive security tooling. Contributors are expected to hold
the same standard online and in how they talk about, and use, the software:
authorization comes before capability, and "I was only testing" is not an excuse.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct and technical. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Give and accept specific, actionable review feedback.
- Credit others' work, and say when an idea came from somewhere else.
- Assume good faith until the evidence says otherwise.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Encouraging or assisting unauthorized use of the tool against systems nobody
  authorized you to test.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or bad-faith reporting.

## Scope

This applies in repository spaces (issues, PRs, discussions) and when you are
representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer listed in `pyproject.toml`. Reports are handled
confidentially. Responses range from a private warning to a permanent ban,
depending on severity and pattern.
